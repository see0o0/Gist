## English (en-US)

# Privacy Policy for TaskFlow AI

**Effective date:** July 27, 2026  
**Contact:** cchhll0210@gmail.com

TaskFlow AI is a Chrome extension for creating and managing tasks, due dates, and reminders. This Privacy Policy explains how TaskFlow AI handles information when you use the extension.

## 1. Task data is stored locally

Your task data, including task titles, descriptions, completion status, due dates, priorities, and reminder times, is stored locally in Chrome storage on your device. TaskFlow AI does not upload task content to our servers.

## 2. Anonymous usage analytics

TaskFlow AI uses Mixpanel to receive anonymous or pseudonymous usage analytics. These analytics help us understand how features are used and improve the extension.

Analytics may include feature interaction events, extension-related technical information, app language, and a randomly generated anonymous identifier. Task titles, task descriptions, reminder text, and other task content are not sent to Mixpanel.

## 3. Chrome permissions

TaskFlow AI requests only permissions needed for its core functions:

- `storage` stores tasks and language preferences locally.
- `alarms` schedules task reminders.
- `notifications` displays reminder notifications.
- `sidePanel` provides the task workspace in Chrome’s Side Panel.

## 4. Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell task data. Task content is not shared with third parties. Anonymous usage analytics are processed by Mixpanel solely to help improve TaskFlow AI.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

You can delete individual tasks within the extension. Removing TaskFlow AI from Chrome removes locally stored extension data from your browser. Anonymous analytics are retained by Mixpanel according to its applicable service settings and policies.

## 6. Security

Because task content is stored locally in your browser, its protection also depends on the security of your device and Chrome profile. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when TaskFlow AI changes. We will update the effective date when material changes are made.

## 8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact cchhll0210@gmail.com.
