# PetDex Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

PetDex is a lightweight pet discovery and collection game that runs entirely in a supported browser's sidebar.

## Information PetDex Stores

PetDex stores only locally generated game information and preferences needed for the product to work:

- Discovered pet identifiers and first-discovery dates
- Number of completed explorations
- Daily discovery bonus state
- Selected interface language
- Internal storage schema version

This information is stored in the browser extension's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on the user's device.

## Information PetDex Does Not Collect

PetDex does not collect or transmit:

- Names, email addresses, account identifiers, or other personal details
- Authentication, financial, payment, health, or location information
- Browsing history, visited URLs, website content, form data, or communications
- Analytics events, advertising identifiers, or tracking information

PetDex has no account system, backend, analytics service, advertising network, or payment service.

## Data Sharing

PetDex does not sell, rent, share, or transmit game progress or preferences to the developer or to third parties.

## Permissions

PetDex uses the `sidePanel` permission to display the game in the browser sidebar. It uses the `storage` permission to keep the user's collection and preferences on the device.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Local PetDex data remains on the device until the user clears the extension's stored data or uninstalls PetDex. No remote copy is retained.

## Changes

If PetDex's data practices change, this policy and the applicable extension store disclosures will be updated before the new practices are introduced.

## Contact

Users can contact the publisher through the support channel provided on the applicable PetDex store listing.
